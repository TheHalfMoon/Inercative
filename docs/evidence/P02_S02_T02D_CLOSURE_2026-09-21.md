## 3. What was delivered

The domain layer of `@ineractive/product-graph` now carries locale and internationalization
semantics:

- the `localeconfig` node kind with required `defaultLocale` and `supportedLocales` and optional
  `fallbackLocale`, `formattingLocale`, `rtlLocales`, and `userSelectable`;
- the exported and documented locale-tag subset (`DOMAIN_LOCALE_TAG_PATTERN`) with the
  `isAcceptedLocaleTag` predicate, recorded as an Ineractive validation subset rather than a claim
  of full BCP-47 conformance;
- the stable node-phase error codes `DOMAIN_INVALID_LOCALE_TAG` and
  `DOMAIN_UNSUPPORTED_LOCALE_REFERENCE`, covering the default, fallback, formatting, and
  right-to-left positions.

The validator is a pure function of the document: it does not consult the host process locale,
wall-clock time, randomness, the network, or a model, which Jev's independent checks
(`environment_dependence` `noul 0.06`, `determinism_risk` `noul 0.19`) support.

## 4. Boundaries recorded honestly

Two semantics that the task's required surface names are deliberately **not** delivered here and are
recorded as out of scope rather than half-modelled:

- translated content and non-translatable identifiers, which belong with content and page
  semantics that the Product Graph does not model yet;
- time-zone configuration, which `docs/canonical/ROADMAP.md` groups with locale semantics but
  which belongs with scheduling and runtime semantics.

No framework adapter (for example a Next.js i18n library) is introduced, consistent with
`docs/canonical/FRONTEND_DESIGN_TOOLCHAIN.md` §19, which requires locale and RTL semantics to live
in the Product Graph with any framework package qualified separately as an adapter.
